# Inbox

Temporary capture for questions, raw observations, links, and ideas that are not yet classified or verified.

- Keep entries short and mark uncertain claims as *unverified*.
- Review periodically; move enduring reference knowledge to `30-resources/`, actionable outcomes to `10-projects/`, or ongoing commitments to `20-areas/`.
- Archive rather than silently deleting useful historical notes.
- Never capture credentials, sensitive personal information, or confidential company material.

See the [classification guide](../docs/knowledge-conventions.md).
